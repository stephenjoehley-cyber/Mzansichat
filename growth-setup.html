The real work behind it

We build the system. Then we run two kinds of marketing.

Not another subscription you have to learn. You work with us on your business priorities and approve the message; we build, test, organise and run the agreed WhatsApp operation.

01 · Setup

Make every agreed entry point work

Set up the official WhatsApp messaging environment with WhatChimp. Connect up to five agreed placements across channels such as your website, Google, Facebook, Instagram and QR materials. Track sources and test the journeys.

02 · Permission & people

Build an audience you can actually contact

Clean up to 1,500 existing records from one agreed format, handle names carefully and record YES/STOP choices. An old phone number alone is not marketing permission. Enquiries reach your actual staff.

03 · Organic acquisition campaigns

12 tailored posts — each with its own way into WhatsApp

Each month we develop campaign ideas, write 12 ready-to-publish posts in total for your agreed channels, adapt the copy and standard branded creative to each placement, and prepare an individual trackable WhatsApp opt-in link for each post. Posts help new people discover you without compulsory ad spend.

04 · Subscriber marketing campaigns

Approved broadcasts to the right customers

Separately from the public posts, we write and prepare up to two WhatsApp marketing broadcasts per month for eligible opted-in subscribers. We handle the Meta template submission, audience segmentation, safe personalisation, seed testing, scheduling and approved sends; then monitor replies, delivery, opt-outs and what worked.

Throughout the month: we maintain opt-in and STOP records, review source and post-level responses where identifiable, resolve routine flow issues, keep your audience organised, and report useful results and next actions. We manage the posting schedule and publication through agreed channels and access arrangements. Clients can also share or publish the content themselves whenever they prefer.

The next chapter · Optional

Then, when the system is ready, turn up the volume.

Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) ads let you run targeted Facebook and Instagram advertising that starts a WhatsApp conversation rather than sending people to a complicated landing page.

We don't recommend buying more traffic into a broken journey. First prove that the enquiry arrives, consent is captured correctly, staff know what to do, and the results can be assessed. Then we can consider carefully controlled paid tests.

CTWA ad spend, creative beyond the standard scope and ad management are separately quoted and approved. They are not part of the R3,495 monthly managed WhatsApp service.

Straight answers

The practical questions.

No technical jargon, no surprise ads budget and no claims of guaranteed sales.

Do I need to spend money on Facebook or Instagram ads?

No. We start with the website, Google listing, posts, social channels, community presence and customer interactions you already have. The service fees and approved WhatsApp marketing-message charges still apply. Paid CTWA advertising is optional and considered later.

What do the 12 monthly posts and two WhatsApp broadcasts mean?

They are two different activities within the managed monthly service. We create and manage scheduling and publication of 12 approved, channel-tailored organic posts in total each month, with tracked WhatsApp opt-in links, through the agreed channels and access arrangements. Clients can also post or share the material themselves if they choose. Separately, we prepare and manage up to two approved WhatsApp Marketing-template broadcasts to eligible opted-in subscribers. Your business approves customer-facing content and remains responsible for customer replies and bookings.

Can you market to my existing customer list?

Only where suitable marketing permission has been obtained. We can clean the agreed historical records, but having a mobile number does not itself authorise promotional WhatsApp messages. We help new customers opt in voluntarily through appropriate channels and respect STOP requests.

What exactly costs extra?

Meta's WhatsApp marketing-template delivery fees are passed on at cost following your approval. Optional paid CTWA advertising and its management, general day-to-day social account administration beyond the agreed monthly posts, content beyond the agreed 12 posts, extra broadcast sends, advanced creative, extra numbers or subscription capacity, and out-of-scope work require a separate quotation and approval.

Does the system confirm appointments automatically?

No. It helps customers enquire and passes booking requests to your nominated staff. Your team checks availability and confirms appointments.

Will I need another WhatsApp number?

Often a dedicated marketing number is appropriate, allowing your current booking WhatsApp to continue. We agree the number and account arrangement for your business before setup; we do not assume the existing line can be reused unchanged.

What is CTWA, and when would we start?

CTWA stands for Click-to-WhatsApp ads: targeted Meta advertisements that open a WhatsApp conversation. We consider this paid second phase after the organic journeys, consent handling, handoffs and measurement have been tested. Ad budget and management fees are agreed separately.

Are bookings or sales guaranteed?

No. Results depend on your offer, the reach and relevance of your existing channels, customer demand and your team's response. We can implement and manage the agreed system, but no honest provider can guarantee enquiries, bookings or revenue.

How long does setup take, and can I cancel?

Timing depends on getting your business information, authenticated Meta access, staff arrangements and message approvals. We agree a realistic delivery plan and the monthly service's cancellation terms in writing before work begins.
