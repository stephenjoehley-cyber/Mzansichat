This policy covers MzansiChat's own business enquiries and the personal information we process when delivering our WhatsApp growth service. A client business may also have its own privacy notice covering its customers.

01

Who we are and our role

MzansiChat is a WhatsApp customer-growth service operated by Contempo Curations (Pty) Ltd, based in Johannesburg, South Africa. In this notice, “we” and “us” refer to MzansiChat and that company.

When you contact MzansiChat directly, we decide how to use that enquiry information and act as the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). When we manage customer data and WhatsApp marketing for a client business, that client generally decides why its customers’ information is processed. MzansiChat works on the client’s instructions as its operator for that activity.

02

Information we handle

Enquiries: your WhatsApp number, name if shared, business name, messages, and information you choose to tell us about your needs.

Client implementation: authorised business details, service information, approved campaign material, account and workflow settings, and the customer-contact files a client supplies for cleaning or configuration.

Customer interactions: WhatsApp numbers and messages, customer-provided first names, consent and unsubscribe choices, source/channel or individual post identifiers, campaign delivery and response records, and booking outcomes where the client makes these available.

Website: basic technical information, such as browser and connection details, may be processed by our hosting or content providers when you load our pages.

03

Why we use it

To answer enquiries, discuss and deliver requested services, build and test WhatsApp customer journeys, prepare marketing content, manage approved campaigns, monitor performance and troubleshoot issues.

To record marketing permission, apply opt-outs and suppressions, help the client keep its database accurate, and keep appropriate business, security and legal records.

We do not treat a number in a historical contact list, or a single service enquiry, as automatic permission to receive ongoing WhatsApp marketing. Promotional messages are sent only where the required permission and other applicable conditions are satisfied.

04

WhatsApp marketing and your choices

A customer may initiate a WhatsApp conversation from a website, Google listing, social post, community post or QR code. A source-specific link can help us and our client understand which channel or post led to the conversation.

Where marketing opt-in is requested, the customer can choose whether to receive future promotional WhatsApp messages. The opt-in decision is recorded separately from an ordinary enquiry. Client-approved campaign messages are sent to eligible audiences using the applicable Meta-approved templates.

You can withdraw marketing permission or object to direct marketing at any time. Reply STOP to a participating client's promotional WhatsApp messages, or contact that business directly. We retain the minimum suppression information needed to respect the request. Opting out of marketing does not prevent necessary responses to a service enquiry.

05

Platforms and service providers

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) and WhatChimp to support the WhatsApp journeys and messaging services described above. Information may be processed by these providers and their authorised infrastructure to deliver the service.

Our website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages and may load Google Fonts.

We may use other approved technical providers where necessary to deliver a client engagement. We do not sell customer lists or personal information. We share information only for legitimate service delivery, with authorised recipients, or where legally required.

06

Storage, security and international processing

Some service providers operate infrastructure outside South Africa. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we seek to use appropriate safeguards and a basis permitted by POPIA. We do not promise that all platform data remains inside South Africa.

We use proportionate security measures such as restricted access, account controls, backups where appropriate and handling procedures for opt-outs and sensitive records. No online system is completely risk-free. If a security incident requires notification under POPIA, the relevant parties will be notified as required by law.

07

How long information is kept

We retain personal information only while reasonably needed for the purpose collected, the client relationship, lawful retention requirements, or to maintain appropriate records of consent and opt-out requests. Financial and contractual records may need to be held for longer where the law requires it.

Client customer information is handled according to the relevant client’s instructions and applicable law. When no longer required, information is deleted, de-identified or securely disposed of as appropriate, subject to lawful exceptions and the retention controls of third-party platforms.

08

Your privacy rights

Under POPIA, you can ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access where applicable, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the basis, and request deletion where lawful. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing information.

If your request concerns a salon or another business using MzansiChat, that business may be the responsible party. We will help route the request appropriately rather than make decisions about its customer records without authorisation.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may contact the Information Regulator (South Africa) or use its complaints service.

09

Website links, cookies and changes

When you select a WhatsApp link, the WhatsApp/Meta service opens and its own privacy terms apply. Cloudflare and providers of fonts or embedded content may process technical connection data and use technologies required for their services. Your browser provides controls over cookies and website data.

We will update this notice when the website, providers or data-handling practices change materially. The revision date will appear at the top of this page.
