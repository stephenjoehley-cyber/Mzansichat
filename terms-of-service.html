What these terms cover

02

Build & Launch

Our standard new-client setup is advertised at R5,950 once-off, subject to the accepted proposal. It is much more than connecting a WhatsApp number.

The agreed build can include configuring the official Meta/WhatChimp messaging setup; protecting an existing booking number where agreed; processing a historical contact file; separating stored contacts from marketing permission; setting up opt-in, verified first names, STOP and suppression; and building tested WhatsApp customer journeys from agreed public sources.

We also prepare and test source-specific links, enquiry and campaign response flows, human booking handoffs, eligible marketing templates and a Google-review journey where in scope. Practical launch checks, operating guidance and handover form part of the agreed setup.

The standard package is scoped to one agreed WhatsApp number, up to five agreed acquisition channels or placements, and up to 1,500 records from one usable source format. Any unusually complex data repair, additional numbers, custom integrations or additional channels are quoted separately before work begins.

03

Manage & Grow

Our standard managed service is advertised at R3,495 per month and includes the agreed WhatChimp platform subscription for the standard setup, subject to plan capacity and scope stated in the accepted service order. It is not simply a software licence or a bulk-send service.

Organic acquisition: we plan campaign themes, prepare 12 channel-tailored publication-ready posts each month with suitable copy and standard branded creative, and provide individually distinguishable WhatsApp opt-in links where the channel supports them. Publishing is handled by us where authorised access is available; otherwise the client publishes the ready-to-use material according to the agreed calendar.

Subscriber marketing: separately from public posts, we plan, write, prepare, and manage up to two permission-based WhatsApp marketing broadcast campaigns per month. These require the correct eligible audience, the client's approval of business facts and customer-facing copy, any necessary Meta template approval, current spend authorisation and a person available to handle replies. A campaign is not deemed successful simply because messages were sent.

Ongoing work also includes normal monitoring, opt-out and failed-delivery hygiene, relevant source tracking, performance review and practical improvements. Scheduling can change when permissions, client approvals, platform status or a live offer are not ready; we agree a safe alternative rather than send unauthorised marketing.

04

Prices and additional costs

The advertised R5,950 setup and R3,495 monthly service are our standard new-client prices, not a change to previously accepted client-specific pricing. MzansiChat is not currently VAT-registered and does not add VAT to its own fees.

Meta marketing-message delivery charges are separate. Applicable charges depend on Meta's current rules and rates and the actual messaging activity. We will provide a current estimate and obtain the client's approval before a chargeable production campaign. No advertising spend is required for the standard organic growth programme.

Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) advertising is an optional later stage once the organic and inbound journeys are tested. Advertising budgets and campaign management are separately proposed and approved; no automatic percentage-of-spend fee applies merely by visiting this website.

Other additions—such as advanced creative production, additional broadcast campaigns, extra platform capacity, additional numbers, custom CRM or integrations—are discussed and priced separately. Payment dates and accepted payment methods are confirmed in each proposal or invoice. We will not introduce undisclosed compulsory software fees.

05

Client inputs, approvals and everyday operation

We prepare the technical setup, proposed copy, campaign plans and QA work. The client supplies or confirms the genuine business facts needed for customers: services, prices, special offers, opening hours, booking arrangements and capacity.

The client approves new customer-facing offers and campaign content, authorises planned chargeable sends and appoints a person to answer customer enquiries. Customers' bookings, prices and availability are only confirmed by the client's authorised team unless a separate verified system and responsibility are expressly agreed.

Clients must not use the service to send unsolicited or unlawful marketing, and must tell us promptly about contact opt-outs, outdated offers or relevant service changes. We implement and maintain the agreed safeguards; we do not transfer all operational or legal responsibility to the client simply because the system uses the client's branding.

06

Customer data, marketing permission and ownership

The client retains the rights it lawfully holds in its customer data, business name, brand assets and supplied materials. MzansiChat accesses and processes customer records only as needed for the authorised service, following the agreed instructions and applicable privacy requirements.

A contact saved in a phone, imported from a historical list or sending an ordinary enquiry does not automatically become a marketing subscriber. We maintain agreed permission, unsubscribe and suppression controls, and use only recipients who satisfy the relevant WhatsApp, Meta and legal marketing requirements.

We use WhatChimp and the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta for the contracted messaging workflows. Their systems and data-processing terms apply where relevant. More information is in our Privacy Policy.

The client should control its own business assets and Meta ownership wherever the platform permits. On a normal handover, we provide the agreed client materials, available contact exports, configuration notes and operating guidance in a reasonably usable form, subject to access rights and the export capabilities of the relevant service. We never promise to export records a third-party platform cannot provide.

07

Platform availability and outcomes

Meta, WhatChimp, social networks and other providers may change pricing, policies, eligibility, service limits or availability. We will explain material changes affecting the agreed service and seek the safest practical solution rather than silently remove a purchased outcome or incur additional costs without approval.

We do not guarantee any fixed number of new subscribers, enquiries, bookings, reviews or sales. Results depend on audience demand, offers, posting consistency, client response times, customer choices and the behaviour of independent platforms. We report measurable activity and commercially meaningful outcomes where suitable evidence is available.

No automated workflow will promise a live booking, discount or price that the client has not confirmed.

MzansiChat retains rights in its independently developed methods, reusable workflow patterns, operating procedures and general tools, except where a separate written agreement provides otherwise. We do not claim ownership of the client's customer relationships or contact list.

Any approved reference must remain accurate; commercially sensitive customer records are not published.
